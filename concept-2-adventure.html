FAQ

Straight answers.

Can you guarantee Mahi Mahi?

No. Season, weather, current, sea conditions and luck all matter.

Do you chase the dolphins?

No. Diyo avoids aggressive chasing and forced close encounters. The dolphins decide the distance.

Is the trip private?

The main tours are designed as private trips for your own small group, subject to boat capacity.

What if the weather is bad?

Safety comes first. Diyo will discuss another time or date if sea conditions are unsuitable.

Can children join?

Usually yes, depending on age, conditions and safety equipment. Ask before booking.

What should I bring?

Sun protection, light clothing, a dry bag and motion-sickness medication if needed.

Where do we meet?

Diyo confirms the Lovina meeting point directly on WhatsApp.

Can you pick us up?

Pickup and drop-off in Lovina and nearby areas can be arranged.

Can we cook the fish afterwards?

Yes. Ask about Catch & Beach Grill or a simple Balinese cooking experience.

How do I book?

Message Diyo on WhatsApp with your date, group size and preferred trip.

Ready to ask Diyo?

Send your date, group size and the trip you have in mind.
