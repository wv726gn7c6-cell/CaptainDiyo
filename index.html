Frequently asked questions

Quick Answers

What time does the tour start?

Choose a sunrise departure around 5:30 AM or a later morning departure around 8:30 AM. Diyo confirms the exact meeting time on WhatsApp.

How many people can join?

The listed price is for a private boat for up to 4 guests.

What is included?

The private boat, Diyo as captain, the dolphin tour and water.

Do you guarantee dolphin sightings?

No. These are wild dolphins, so sightings cannot be guaranteed.

Do you chase the dolphins?

No aggressive chasing. If dolphins move away, that behaviour is respected.

How can I book?

Message Diyo directly on WhatsApp with your preferred date and group size.
