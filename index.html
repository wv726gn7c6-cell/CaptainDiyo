FAQ

Straight answers before you book.

Can you guarantee Mahi Mahi?

No. Mahi Mahi are wild fish. Season, weather, current, sea conditions and luck all matter.

Do you chase the dolphins?

No. Diyo aims to observe dolphins respectfully and avoids aggressive chasing or forcing close encounters.

Is the trip private?

Yes. The listed prices are for the private boat, up to 4 guests.

What happens if the sea is unsafe?

Safety comes first. Diyo will discuss a different time or date if conditions are not suitable.

Can children join?

Usually yes, depending on age, sea conditions and available safety equipment. Ask Diyo before booking.

Where do we meet?

Diyo confirms the exact Lovina meeting point on WhatsApp before the trip.
